Every power sits on one ladder. Each rung is cut from the rung above, and a lower rung can never fully override a higher one on the same line.

  • The planes (dreams, thought, time) connect only to their Godlings, but they're the ground everyone's experience stands on. Every dream happens on the dream plane.

Rank caps reach. A character's rank is their highest engraved stat. Rank I reaches a Soul, II a Host, III a Ward, IV a City, V a Realm, VI a Dominion or a Face, VII the Planet. Only Godlings can reach the planet. That's why the Storyborn fear them.

Caps and dangers

  • Every Cut lowers Vitality's maximum for good. It carves the heart.
  • Precision is capped by the true name tier. Selfhood falls as a Storyborn climbs (max = 8 − tier); Draw and Dominion are capped by tier.
  • A Godling's Vessel and Capacity are capped by level. High Rumour early is deadly; high Ripple gets them found; high Whispers means the retainer is close to moving in.
  • A Voided's Edge gates rank: Clear needs 5, Switcher needs 7. Low Stillness means the erasure can consume them. Hollow-born have no Heart.
  • A Fallen's Exposure is 0 until they graft; each graft raises its maximum.

Storyborn物語生

A god caught by The Binding, living in a human host by possession, merging, or as a parasite. Strong now, chained forever.

  • Archetype: tied to one concept. Crossing into another concept costs existence. The archetype can be upgraded, with the Voice's approval.
  • Tier = domain size, and the domain follows them. Abilities work only inside it. Every new Storyborn starts at tier 1.
  • Drift: the higher they climb, the more the concept replaces their personality.
  • Gifts: one per follower, per Storyborn. Followers' deeds can upgrade the archetype.
  • Costs: story (acting against the archetype) and existence.
  • The look: the archetype symbol on the back, with rings around it and glyphs flowing along the body. They can show or hide it. The rings show which ability is active: they rotate for small ones and lift off the back into a halo for big ones. Shifting glyphs mean an innate passive. When they run low, the rings and glyphs slow down.
  • Skills: merge and parasite hosts keep their skills, but using one outside the concept costs story. Possession hosts lose them, because the person is gone.

Era 1 People名

Everyone born in Era 1 carries a dormant name. When the Voice of the World wakes it, their core skill wakes with it.

  • Who wakes: anyone can, but the Voice only wakes people it finds interesting, by its own taste: freedom, bullies and the people who stand up to them, the weak growing strong, tragedies. Most people don't know this is the rule.
  • The name is the title. It refines with mastery (Thief → Benevolent Thief → … tier 7). Each refinement rewrites the name on the heart, and it hurts badly.
  • Skills: a core, up to 7 branches (one per title tier), and derivatives that grow deep through heavy use, capped by the title tier. No two people share a skill.
  • Invoking: everyone has their own style. Some recite the story behind the skill, some use hand signs, and the closest to their own story do it silently.

The heart

The tank

The heart holds the energy, breathed in from the air. Spending makes it race; resting refills it.

The rings

Only the name is written on the heart. With training, skills are lifted off it into invisible rings around it, which hold each skill's story. Only the user, and rare seers, can perceive them.

The gears

Skill rings spin fast. The name turns very slowly, but every use nudges it. Chaining skills spins the name up, and the friction and heat damage the heart.

The cost

Cores and branches cost energy: overuse leaves you drained and shaking. Pushing a derivative past your tier also strains the heart. (subject to change)

The Cut刻

The only way to pay for more power. You give the world a piece of yourself, and it carves a permanent boon into you.

Here lies what I was.
I give it: my fear, my mother's face.
I bind it, I bury it, I nail it down.
Carve it, World, and read it back to me.
Let the grave of gods hold one line more.
Epitaph.
  • At "Epitaph" the Voice speaks from inside them. The exchange takes a fraction of a second, and only they feel it. Others sense the energy afterwards.
  • The Voice asks what they desire, then decides whether the offering is enough. They can't refuse what it asks for.
  • Cost: an emotion, a memory or bond, or a piece of yourself or your existence. Whatever is taken rots or withers into energy, and the world feeds on it.
  • Gain: the Cut itself, a named boon that always works but reaches full strength only when they act on the desire they spoke. (subject to change)
  • Every Cut also carves the heart. People can die right after receiving the boon.
  • Storyborn rarely Cut. They're too proud. The Voice narrows their archetype, takes a piece of them, and pulls them in among the gods sealed in the books, the bitter ones who live inside the Voice.
  • Anyone with a dormant name can make a Cut. The Fallen can't.

The Voided虚

The people of the Hollow Reach, who follow Val. Their warriors carry his blessing: a binding mark, a light piece of Val himself, that lets them hold erasure in stasis. They are the weapon: close-combat assassins, not frontline soldiers.

  • Val holds the final say over every blessing. Only he can lift the lid completely and turn a Voided into a bomb that erases a settlement. If Val dies, every blessed Voided explodes.
  • The Black covers only the surface. Weapons that touch it are erased. While it's up, energy can't reach the heart, so skills don't grow, and the Voice can't see them. Skilled Voided live double lives.

  • Children born in the Hollow Reach have no dormant name.

Items物

Items carry powers the way people do, but an item's power is fitted, not earned. A god, the Voice or a Storyborn grafts it on. Every item has a rank and a tier, and some have states.

Rank and tier

Rank is how far the item's effect reaches right now. Tier is who could make it. They are separate: a god can make a Divine item that reaches only one soul.

A rank sets the lowest tier allowed; a higher tier is always fine. Rank follows what the item can do now: if it loses the binding that gave it reach, its rank drops.

States

Item states are its condition, such as Sealed, then Broken. Usage states are what part of a process it is in, such as Dormant, then Active. An item may also have a third track, named for it. A track can be one-way. Not every item has states.

Bindings

Abilities grafted onto an item by gods, the Voice or Storyborn. A binding changes what the item does and has no cost, because the power is part of the item. It has a name, a source, an effect, and the reach it gives.

Blessings

Given to people only, and only when both sides agree. They are stable. A blessing may have:

  • Price: what holding it always takes.
  • Terms: the condition to keep.
  • Breach: what happens if the terms are broken.
  • Lasts until: how it stops, normally.

Example: Warping lets you step instantly to any place in the giver's domain that you have been before. It lasts until your service ends.

Curses

A curse changes what a person or an item is. It is not something a god or a Storyborn applies. It is a plain cost that follows from something: an experiment that came out unstable, power pushed past what it can bear, something altered that should have been left alone. Experimenting with what you do not understand is how curses are born.

A curse needs no consent and cannot be removed. It has no cost and no end of its own: the curse is the cost. It is one of four types: Mutation changes the physical make-up of something, Enhancement stretches a trait past its natural limit, Distortion changes how something fundamentally behaves, Fragmentation splits what should be whole.

Every curse passes through three stages, after an initial state in which nothing seems wrong yet:

  • Awakening: the curse begins to show itself. Something is wrong, and the first signs appear.
  • Fluctuation: its true nature shows. Its form shifts and the harm is deep.
  • Stabilization: it settles into its final form. This is not recovery or control. A curse stays a curse.

Each curse names and describes its own stages.

The Fallen堕

The survivors with no dormant name. The Voice can't read them, so the Storyborn can't sense them. Their power is tech, numbers, and later magic-tech.

  • Tech runs on the energy in the air. It can be copied, shared and built in bulk, and it costs no heart strain. With tanks and missiles it reaches a settlement.
  • Grafting (magic-tech): Nera learns to activate the god-words on Storyborn bodies and graft them onto gauntlets, weapons, armour or skin, powered like the Voided stations. A graft works on contact or through the thing it's engraved into: a reality seal can turn ground to mud or a person to stone, and the same seal on a bullet works on impact. What's possible depends on what that Storyborn controlled.
  • The price: a grafted Fallen carries part of a dead deity and stops being invisible. Artificial dormant names can be made, but they never fit the wearer properly.
  • Two concepts in one graft collide and explode, killing the wearer. Chaos appears for a moment and offers something for the offering, like a ritual. Each bomb needs words from two Storyborn corpses, Chaos twists what it's asked for, and the blast tops out at a settlement.

Three kinds of ruin, three scars
